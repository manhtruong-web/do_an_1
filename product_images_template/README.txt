HUONG DAN GUI ANH SAN PHAM SMARTMART

1. Them cac file anh JPG/JPEG, PNG hoac WebP vao thu muc Images/Products.
2. Mo anh-san-pham.csv bang Excel. Dien cot TenFileAnh theo dung MaSP.
   Vi du: Images/Products/sua-vinamilk.jpg
3. TenSP va MaSP da duoc lap san; khong sua MaSP de tranh gan nham anh.
4. Nen dung duong dan tuong doi trong goi ZIP, bat dau bang Images/Products/.
5. Nen file zip nay va gui lai trong khung chat. Hay chi dien anh khi chac chan
   do la dung san pham; de trong neu khong co anh phu hop.

Goi nay gom 200 san pham. Thu muc anh dang de trong, khong tao anh gia.
